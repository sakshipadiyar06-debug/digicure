AIR-GAPPED HARDWARE VAULT

DigiCure

An unhackable hardware password vault for your ESP32-S3. Provision firmware, upload updates, and manage credentials securely via Web Serial.

Web Serial is not supported in this browser. Please open DigiCure in Chrome, Edge, Opera, or Brave.
1

Flash MicroPython Engine

Initialize your ESP32-S3 hardware with the MicroPython runtime. First time only — this erases the board, including any saved vault.

Your browser doesn't support Web Serial. Web Serial is only allowed over HTTPS or localhost.
2

Upload DigiCure Application

Uploads main.py to your connected device and restarts it. Your existing vault is kept. Connect the device in step 3 first.

3

Hardware Vault Manager

Connect over Web Serial, then log in with your PIN — here or on the device itself. Adding or deleting a credential always needs a tap on the device.
