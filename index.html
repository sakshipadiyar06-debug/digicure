DigiCure

Install and manage your offline password vault from the browser. Your PIN and passwords never touch the internet.

This needs Web Serial: use Chrome, Edge, Brave or Opera on a computer.

1 · Flash MicroPython (first time only)

Connect the ESP32-S3 over USB. This erases the board, including any saved vault.

Browser not supported. Open this page over HTTPS.

2 · Install / update the DigiCure app

Uploads main.py to the board and restarts it. Your vault is kept.

3 · Manage your vault

Not connected

Log in by entering your PIN on the device itself. The website only unlocks its tools once the device is unlocked.

Add a password

Saving, and deleting, must be approved with a tap on the device.
